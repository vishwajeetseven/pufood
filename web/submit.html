Capture the Menu and Enhance PUFood for Everyone!

We are excited to introduce our "Capture the Menu and Enhance PUFood for Everyone!" initiative, aimed at improving food accessibility across Parul University outlets. By capturing and submitting clear images of food menus from various campus outlets, you help create a more convenient and informative platform for students and staff.

Why Participate?

  • Make Food Choices Easier – Your contributions ensure that all PU outlets have updated menu listings, making it simple for everyone to find their preferred meals.
  • Enhance Campus Dining – Help fellow students and faculty explore all available food options across the university.
  • Be a Valued Contributor – Join a growing community working to make PUFood the go-to food discovery platform for Parul University.
  • Earn Recognition & Rewards – Valid submissions may be featured on our Contributors Page, along with other perks.

How It Works?

  1. Capture a clear and readable photo of a menu from any Parul University outlet.
  2. Ensure the outlet is not already listed on PUFood.
  3. Submit your photo using our online form.
  4. If approved, your contribution will be added, and you can check your rewards on our Contributors Page.

Your small effort can make a big difference! Help us build the most reliable campus food discovery platform.
