About PUFood

PUFood is a comprehensive food price comparison platform tailored to the needs of students and faculty at Parul University. My mission is to simplify the process of finding the best food deals across various campus outlets, making your dining choices easier and more informed.

My Mission

At PUFood, my primary goal is to enhance the dining experience for everyone on campus. I understand the challenges of finding affordable and desirable food options, especially in a bustling university environment. By providing a platform that compares prices and menus, I aim to save you time and money while ensuring you get the meals you crave.

About Me

I am Aditya Yadav, a Computer Science student at Parul University. The inspiration for PUFood came from my personal experiences and the shared struggles of fellow students in finding convenient and affordable food options on campus.

I noticed that students often relied on word-of-mouth or physically visiting multiple outlets to compare menus and prices. This process was time-consuming and inefficient, leading me to develop a digital solution that centralizes this information.

With PUFood, I strive to provide a user-friendly platform that not only helps students make informed food choices but also fosters a sense of community by sharing insights and recommendations.

The Inspiration

The idea for PUFood was born out of my daily struggles of deciding what to eat on campus. Often, I would crave specific foods but had no easy way to know which outlets served them or at what prices. This led to frequent requests for menu pictures from friends or wandering between outlets in search of desired meals.

PUFood addresses these challenges by offering a centralized platform where students can easily find and compare food options, making the dining experience more convenient and enjoyable.
